      Rules

      • Clearing a mission pays XP and the same number of credits.
      • XP raises your level. It only drops when you admit a slip. Credits buy homes and cars.
      • Your stock rises 1% per 25 XP, jumps on milestones (perfect day, streaks, level up, boss, purchases) and falls up to 6% for a day with missed missions.
      • Each streak day adds 5% XP, up to 50%. Miss a day and the streak resets.
      • You can plan future days but only clear today or earlier.
      • The 3D models are stylized stand-ins. The showroom photos show the real thing.
