            How scoring works
            • The lock-in runs 1 to 31 December. Before it opens you can plan; nothing earns XP until day 1, and nothing counts after day 31.
            • Three objectives: your first €100 earned online, the piano concert and the winter exams.
            • Log real income only. Each euro pays 5 XP and 5 credits. Reaching €1, €10, €25, €50 and €100 pays bonus XP and lifts your stock.
            • Missions pay 10, 25, 50 or 100 XP and the same in credits. Each streak day adds 5% XP, up to 50%. Miss a day and the streak resets.
            • $LOCK moves smoothly. A mission lifts it 1% per 25 XP, eased in over four hours. Milestones ease in over three hours; a slip crashes it within twenty minutes.
            • After three waking hours with no progress it drifts down 0.2% an hour (11pm to 7am doesn't count). Missed missions cost up to 4% at the end of the day.
            • Major missions pay 1,000 XP and 1,000 credits when complete, and $LOCK reacts. Each prep session pays 25 XP.
            • XP raises your level and only drops when you admit a slip. Credits buy residences and vehicles in the vault.
            • You can plan future days but only complete today or earlier. On a phone, swipe a mission right to complete it.
