          How scoring works
          • The lock-in runs December 1 to 31. One goal: your first €100 earned online.
          • Log real income only. Each euro pays 5 XP and 5 credits. Reaching €1, €10, €25, €50 and €100 pays bonus XP and lifts your stock.
          • Missions pay 10, 25, 50 or 100 XP and the same in credits. Each streak day adds 5% XP, up to 50%. Miss a day and the streak resets.
          • $LOCK rises 1% per 25 XP, jumps on milestones (perfect day, streaks, levels, objectives, purchases) and falls up to 6% for a day with missed missions.
          • Major missions pay 1,000 XP and 1,000 credits when complete and lift your stock 20%. Each prep session pays 25 XP.
          • XP raises your level and only drops when you admit a slip. Credits buy residences and vehicles in the vault.
          • You can plan future days but only complete today or earlier. On a phone, swipe a mission right to complete it.
